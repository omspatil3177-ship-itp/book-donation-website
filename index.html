01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Every book
holds a new beginning.

Our Community Engagement Project brought our team to Alphabet'z Pre-Primary School, where we donated 100 storybooks for young learners. The books included Treasures of Stories, Birbal the Wise, fairy tales, and other storybooks. Our aim was to encourage reading, imagination, and a love of learning.

Through teamwork and community participation, we wanted to make storybooks more accessible and encourage children to discover the joy of reading.
